# Why is the uidA target region not intact?

Generated by scripts/uida_nonintact_check.py (classes in its docstring; internal_gap = both gene ends present but a
part in between missing). Subfolders: upec (738 UPEC genomes), lamp (38 clinical E. coli isolates), ncbi (5,493 RefSeq
complete E. coli genomes). nonintact_check.tsv: per genome the fraction of uidA aligned, best identity, the status of the
5' and 3' end of the gene (complete / contig_end / internal) and the class. No genome is explained by a contig end.
